Install

  1. Laptop. Open this file in Chrome, Edge or Safari and allow the camera and microphone. Nothing else to install.
  2. Phone. Phone browsers only allow the camera on a secure (https) address. Put this one file on any static host, open the link on the phone, then choose Add to Home Screen from the browser menu. It then opens full screen like an app.
  3. No hosting yet. Record a slow-motion clip with the phone's own camera and load it with Analyse a clip. This also gives the best accuracy, because the clip has more frames.

Set up at the net

  1. Put the phone on a tripod behind the bowler's stumps, in line with middle stump, about head height. Both sets of stumps must be in view.
  2. Tap Calibrate and tap four points: base and top of the far middle stump, then base and top of the near middle stump. The app works out the lens, the camera height and the distance from the 22-yard pitch.
  3. Pick the bowler type and a field, then play. Each shot shows on the ground with its speed, direction, launch angle and what it was worth.

Access it uses

  • Camera to follow the ball off the bat.
  • Microphone to time the moment of contact. Audio is analysed on the device and never recorded or uploaded.
  • Screen wake lock so the phone does not sleep mid-session. No prompt.
  • Local storage for your five custom fields, ground size and calibration. No prompt.
  • Not needed: location, contacts, photos, Bluetooth, notifications.

What to trust

  • Direction is the strongest reading. Speed is weaker, and weakest for balls hit straight back at the camera.
  • Runs are a physics estimate from a few metres of ball flight, as in every net simulator.
  • Every ball shows a confidence level, and you can correct any reading with the sliders.
  • The sound filter throws out voices, music and low thuds. A sharp clap or a second bat nearby can still pass it, which is why the camera check is on by default.

Dimensions used

  • Pitch 22 yards (20.12 m). Stumps 71.1 cm high.
  • Inner ring: 30-yard (27.43 m) semicircles from each middle stump, joined by straight lines.
  • Boundary: 65 to 90 yards (59.4 to 82.3 m) in men's internationals. The default here is an average 65 m square and 70 m straight.
